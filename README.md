# AtliQ Reports – Excel-Based Business Performance Report

## Executive Summary
The **AtliQ Reports** project is a business intelligence initiative built entirely in **Microsoft Excel**, focused on turning raw operational and financial data into insightful, easy-to-use reports. It delivers **customer-level analysis**, **market performance evaluation**, and **profit & loss breakdowns** across time and geography.

The reports were designed for stakeholders to explore trends, identify performance gaps, and guide strategic decisions — without needing any technical tools beyond Excel. With a balance of data structure, clean visuals, and thoughtful storytelling, this project showcases how powerful insights can be delivered through simplicity and precision.

## Objective
To develop a set of structured, interactive Excel reports for **AtliQ Hardwares** that provide clear visibility into:

- Customer sales growth and trends
- Market-wise performance versus targets
- Monthly and market-based financial health (Profit & Loss)

Each report aims to empower decision-makers with data clarity and actionable insights.

## 📁 Key Reports
### 1. 🧑‍💼 Customer Performance Report
#### Overview
- Evaluated customer-wise sales from FY 2019 to Fiscal Year 2021.
- Tracked growth rates, uncovering rapid expansion across retail and digital channels.

|Key Insights| Recomendations|
|-------------|----------|
|<li>Electricalslytical (431.1%), AtliQ Exclusive (392.6%), and Girias (419.3%) showed explosive growth.</li> <li>E-commerce platforms like Amazon and Flipkart recorded consistent upward trends, marking them as dependable long-term partners. </li><li>High-growth yet previously under-the-radar clients like Expression and Propel emerged as potential strategic accounts.</li>|<li>Invest more in high-potential customers via personalized sales campaigns. </li><li>Re-engage mid-tier customers to boost retention and lifetime value. </li><li>Use these trends to design tiered loyalty or partnership programs.</li>

#### Impact
- These findings help in customer segmentation for strategic engagement — distinguishing high-growth clients from flat performers.

### 2. 🌍 Market Performance vs Sales Targets
#### Overview
- Compared actual sales to FY 2021 market targets across 20+ countries.

|Key Insights| Recomendations|
|------------|--------------|
|<li>Despite overall revenue growth, most markets fell short of their targets — e.g., Canada (-14.5%), Poland (-18.1%), and Indonesia (-12.9%).</li><li>India, while under target by -5.9%, remained the largest and most stable contributor.</li>|<li>Focus on sales enablement and demand forecasting models in underperforming regions.</li><li>Conduct market-level SWOT analyses to identify execution barriers.</li>|

#### Impact
- Highlights misalignment between forecasting and execution.
- Indicates potential overestimation of capacity in emerging or volatile markets.

### 3. 📆 Profit & Loss Report – Monthly
#### Overview
- Captured Net Sales, COGS, and Gross Margins across fiscal months and quarters from Fiscal Year 2019–2021.

|Key Insights| Recomendations|
|----------|-------------|
|<li>Noticeable sales seasonality around Q3 and Q4 (Nov–Dec), with spikes across all fiscal years.</li><li>Gross Margins remained relatively consistent across months (average ~36–41%), despite fluctuating sales volume.</li><li>FY 2021 witnessed a significant revenue leap — Net Sales rose from 196.7M in 2020 to 598.9M in 2021.</li>| <li>Leverage peak seasons with targeted promotions and inventory readiness.</li><li>Review operational costs in slower months (e.g., Feb, Apr) for margin protection.</li><li>Plan campaigns to flatten demand spikes and smoothen production cycles.</li>| 

#### Impact
- Confirms seasonal sales peaks and validates pricing efficiency via stable GM%.
- Offers clarity on the cost structure’s scalability under volume pressure.

### 4. 🗺️ Profit & Loss Report – By Market
#### Overview
- Broke down FY 2021 financial performance (Net Sales, COGS, GM%) by country.

|Key Insights|Recommendations|
|-------------|------------|
|<li>High-performing markets by GM%: New Zealand (48.2%), Japan (46.5%), UK (45.1%).</li><li>Low-margin regions: Germany (26.2%), Norway (29.5%), and India (32.0%) despite high sales volume.</li><li>Balanced markets (strong sales and healthy margins): USA and South Korea.</li> |  <li>Improve procurement/supply chain efficiency in low-GM markets.</li><li>Replicate operational strategies from high-GM countries like New Zealand and Japan.</li><li>Use this data to inform pricing, promotions, and regional product strategies.</li>|

#### Impact
- Allows benchmarking to assess which markets offer profitable scale vs costly volume.
- Enables prioritization in global expansion or restructuring strategies.
  
## Skills & techniques Applied
### 🔧 Excel-Based Analysis
- Structured ETL workflows using Excel functions and manual checks.
- Built pivot tables and charts for KPI tracking and dynamic filtering.
- Applied calculations for growth %, GM%, YoY variance, and targets gap analysis.

### 🎨 User-Centered Report Design
- Prioritized visual clarity with clean layouts and logical data grouping.
- Designed for non-technical users — intuitive filters, labels, and summaries.
- Focused on “what does this mean?” rather than just “what’s the number?”

### 💡 Analytical & Soft Skills
- Connected numbers to business context and actions.
- Took a systematic approach to identify gaps, risks, and opportunities.
- Built reports that answer real stakeholder questions, not just display data.
